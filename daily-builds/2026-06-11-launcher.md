### r/SideProject

**Title:** I built a compound interest calculator that shows the real (inflation-adjusted) value side-by-side

**Body:**
Most compound interest calculators I tried had one of two problems: they ignored monthly contributions, or they hid the inflation-adjusted value behind a toggle nobody finds.

So I built one that does both. Enter your initial deposit, monthly contribution, rate, years, and compound frequency. Toggle the inflation rate to see the real value in today's dollars. There's a year-by-year schedule that shows exactly when interest overtakes your contributions.

It's free, no signup, runs entirely in your browser. The whole point was to make the most useful version of this calculator I could find, not to gate it.

Happy to take feedback — what would you add?

### r/webdev

**Title:** [Show] A small pure-TS compound interest calculator with a year-by-year schedule

**Body:**
Built a single-purpose calculator to learn more about how compound interest + monthly contributions interact over long horizons. Stack:

- Next.js 16 + React 19 + TypeScript (strict)
- Pure functions in `src/lib/compound-interest.ts`, no React in the core
- Month-by-month simulation so monthly contributions and arbitrary compound frequencies combine correctly
- 14 unit tests for the core (validation, boundary, frequency comparison, inflation)
- Schema.org WebApplication + FAQPage JSON-LD for SEO

The inflation-adjusted real value turned out to be the most interesting part: at 6% growth / 3% inflation over 20 years, the real return is closer to 3% — about half the headline rate.

🔗 https://loancalc-eta.vercel.app/compound-interest-calculator

## 3. Hacker News — Show HN

**Title:** Show HN: Compound Interest Calculator with Inflation-Adjusted Value

**Body:**
I built a free, no-signup compound interest calculator that does three things most calculators skip:

1. Treats monthly contributions as a first-class input (not an afterthought)
2. Shows the inflation-adjusted real value alongside the nominal future balance
3. Includes a year-by-year schedule so you can see exactly when interest overtakes contributions

Tech: Next.js + TypeScript, pure functions for the core math (month-by-month simulation so arbitrary compound frequencies and monthly contributions combine correctly), 14 unit tests, WebApplication + FAQPage schema.org markup for SEO.

The big insight from building it: monthly contributions typically dwarf the impact of the compounding frequency itself. Adding $200/month for 30 years at 7% returns ~$227K (with ~$155K contributed); skipping the monthly contributions and only investing $10K would yield only ~$76K.

Open to feedback — what would you want in a tool like this?
