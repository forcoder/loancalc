# 2026-06-11 Daily Build — Compound Interest Calculator

## 日期: 2026-06-11

## 痛点
Saving/investing users want to know how much their money can grow through compound interest with regular monthly contributions and inflation adjustment. Most existing tools either ignore monthly contributions or hide the real (inflation-adjusted) value.

## 搜索验证
- 主词: "compound interest calculator" — ~50K-100K/月, 高竞争
- 长尾目标: "compound interest calculator with monthly contributions" — ~8K-15K/月, KD < 25
- 二级长尾: "compound interest calculator with inflation" — ~3K-6K/月, KD < 20
- 目标: 主长尾 + 通胀调整差异化

## 工具名
Compound Interest Calculator (复利计算器)

## 路由
`/compound-interest-calculator`

## 核心功能
1. **本金 + 月供 + 年限 + 利率 + 复利频率** → 计算本息合计、总利息、实际购买力（通胀调整）
2. **本金贡献 vs 利息贡献** 拆分（让用户直观看到复利威力）
3. **年化明细表**（每年本金/利息/余额）— 可选 accordion 展开
4. **通胀调整开关**（默认 3%）— 展示名义 vs 实际购买力

- monthlyContribution ($)
- annualRate (%)
- years (1-50)
- compoundFrequency (monthly/quarterly/annually)
- inflationRate (% 默认 3, 0 表示不调整)
